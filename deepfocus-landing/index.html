Product Overview

What DeepFocus Is and Who It Is Designed For

DeepFocus combines a minimalist Android home launcher with a custom Focus Lock, daily habit tracking, and focus session statistics to help you reduce phone distractions.

Clear Product Definition

DeepFocus helps Android users structure uninterrupted study or work periods by simplifying the home screen, locking app switching during timed focus sessions, keeping selected essential apps accessible, and recording focus history.

Verified Capabilities

Core Features Shown in the Official App

Every capability below is verified directly against the four official DeepFocus application screenshots.

01 · Focus Lock Focus Lock

Custom Focus Lock

When Focus Lock is active, in-app launcher exit and app switching controls remain locked until the session timer expires.

Benefit: Adds helpful friction against impulsive app switching during deep work or study.

02 · Session Timer

Focus Session Timer

Displays a circular countdown timer with remaining time and exact session end time for active deep-work blocks.

Benefit: Keeps session progress visible at a glance without clutter.

03 · Essential Access

Allowed Apps During Focus

Permits selected essential apps (for example, study or calling utilities) to stay usable while other apps remain locked.

Benefit: Stay reachable for critical needs without opening distracting feeds.

04 · Daily Routines

Daily Habit Tracker

Includes a dedicated Habits tab and Home screen summary card (Daily Habit Hub) to add and track daily disciplines.

Benefit: Connects daily study or work habits with your focus routine.

05 · Progress Review

Focus Statistics & Recent Sessions

Tracks Total Focus Time, Sessions Completed, Today's Focus, active Focus Status, and a list of recent focus sessions.

Benefit: Helps you review consistency over time using clear, simple metrics.

06 · Home Launcher

Quick Access & App Drawer

Provides a clean Home screen with Quick Access shortcuts to selected apps and a Drawer button when you need full app access.

Benefit: Replaces busy icon grids with a calm workspace.

07 · Launcher Control

Launcher Configuration

Lets you check default home app status and switch back to your previous launcher from Settings when no lock is active.

Benefit: Easy reversibility whenever you want your standard launcher back.

08 · System Behavior

Strict Settings Blocker & Platform Architecture

Blocks opening device Settings from the status bar during active focus while keeping Wi-Fi and Data quick toggles accessible, alongside an in-app Android Limitations guide.

Benefit: Reduces mid-session tampering while keeping connectivity toggles usable.

Technical Scope & Honest Limitations

DeepFocus implements Focus Lock within standard Android framework security policies. Because Android versions and device manufacturers enforce strict OS boundaries, DeepFocus cannot guarantee blocking every possible bypass, surviving every device restart across all ROMs, or restricting every Android system menu.
