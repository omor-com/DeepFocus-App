Privacy Summary & Pre-Launch Draft Policy

Important transparency notice: this draft policy must be reviewed and confirmed against the final DeepFocus Android application build by Muhammed Omor before publication.

Draft Status Notice: The developer of this landing website has not performed an independent security audit or static/dynamic code inspection of the DeepFocus Android APK. Any statement below marked for confirmation must be verified by the product owner (Muhammed Omor) before final release.

1. This Landing Website (Static Site)

This landing website is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site. It does not include user accounts, login forms, contact forms, payment processing forms, or default third-party tracking scripts. The only browser storage used is an optional localStorage entry (deepfocus_lang) to remember whether you selected English or Bangla.

2. External Purchase Destination (SupportKori)

When you click any purchase or "Get DeepFocus" button, you leave this website and visit the official SupportKori listing operated by SupportKori. Any account login, checkout, or payment details entered there are governed by SupportKori's own terms and privacy policy.

3. DeepFocus Android App — Data Collection & Local Storage

[Requires product-owner verification] Based on the visible app screens (Habit Tracker, Focus Statistics, Allowed Apps, and Settings), session history and habit records appear to be stored locally on the user's device. Before publishing this policy as final, the developer should confirm:

  • Whether any personal data, telemetry, or crash reports are transmitted over the network.
  • Whether local data is encrypted or included in Android cloud auto-backup.
  • Whether any third-party analytics, ads, or licensing SDKs are bundled in the APK.

4. Android Permissions Transparency

[Requires product-owner verification] Screen 04 confirms that DeepFocus uses default Home Launcher configuration. Before launch, list every sensitive Android permission declared in AndroidManifest.xml (for example: PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS, SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE, or QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) and explain strictly why each permission is needed.

5. Contact for Privacy Questions

[Requires product-owner confirmation] Add the official developer support email address here prior to launch. Until then, visitors may use the official Contact Seller option on the SupportKori listing.
